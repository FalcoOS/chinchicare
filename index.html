KNOWLEDGE LIBRARY

Care topics

🥬 Food, hay & safe treats

Unlimited good-quality hay is essential. Introduce food changes slowly and avoid sugary, fatty or unsuitable human foods. Ask an exotic-pet veterinarian about individual dietary needs.

🦷 Teeth & chewing

Provide safe chew materials and monitor eating, drooling and droppings. Not eating is urgent for chinchillas and needs prompt veterinary advice.

🛁 Dust baths & coat care

Use suitable chinchilla dust and offer baths for an appropriate, supervised period. Avoid water bathing unless a veterinarian specifically directs it.

🤍 Trust, handling & enrichment

Let your chinchilla approach voluntarily, keep movements calm, and provide hideouts, safe platforms and enrichment. Never force interaction.

🚑 Health, emergencies & vet visits

Track appetite, droppings, behaviour and weight. Sudden appetite loss, breathing difficulty, severe lethargy or bloating warrants urgent veterinary care.

General information only, not individual veterinary advice. Contact a chinchilla-experienced veterinary practice if you notice anything unusual.
